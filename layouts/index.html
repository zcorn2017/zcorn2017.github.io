Research Interests:

I am a PhD student in Plant Biology at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working under the supervision of Prof. Andrew Leakey and Prof. Diwakar Shukla. My research focuses on protein-DNA interactions using computational methods. I hold a BSc (1st Class Hons) in Bioinformatics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.
